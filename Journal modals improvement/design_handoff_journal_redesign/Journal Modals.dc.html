REST 2 · MONTH GRID
Notes: the level is remembered per surface, as the calendar's zoom is. Tapping a dotted day in the grid scrolls the list to that entry rather than filtering — the list stays one continuous thing at both levels. Grid navigation and scrubber navigation are the same act, so changing month in one moves the other.
TURN 2 · THE LIST PAGE, WITHOUT THE MODE TOGGLE
Something that earns the lane
You're right: All entries / By month costs a 48px lane and buys month separators. Both replacements delete the toggle and put navigation in its place — 2a a month scrubber, 2b a month grid. Cards here are 1b's spine cards so the layout is the only variable.
2A
Month scrubber
One continuous, reverse-chronological list — the only order a journal has. The toggle's lane becomes a scrubber: months with entries carry a dot, the current one is the selection pill, tapping jumps the list. Sticky month rule while you scroll.
